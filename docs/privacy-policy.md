# BizNote Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-25

BizNote is a business note and business card management app. The app does not require account registration or login.

## Data Stored by the App

BizNote may store notes, business card information, scanned card images, attachments, event information, meeting information, task information, tags, app settings, and optional location text selected by the user.

This data is stored on the user's device. If the user enables iCloud sync, data may be synced through the user's personal iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit service.

## Event Mode Visitor Information

Event Mode lets the user (for example, a booth operator at an exhibition) collect information from visitors on the user's own device.

Visitor information entered by the visitor or the user may include name, company, job title, email address, mobile and phone numbers, country, a memo, area of interest, whether the visitor requested materials, the date and time the visitor agreed to the privacy notice, a photo of the visitor's business card if one was scanned, and the event the information was collected for. Promotional videos the user selects are copied into the app's storage on the device.

This information is handled like all other data in the app. It is stored on the user's device and, if the user enables iCloud sync, may be synced through the user's personal iCloud account. The developer does not receive, access, or store visitor information, and it is not shared with Google or any advertiser.

The user who collects visitor information is responsible for explaining why it is collected, obtaining the visitor's consent, and handling it according to the laws that apply to them. BizNote shows a consent notice to visitors before they submit their information, and the user can edit its wording in Event Mode settings.

The user can export visitor information as a spreadsheet or PDF, and optionally business card images as a ZIP file, through the system share sheet. The user can also delete visitor information inside the app.

Access to Event Mode management screens can be protected with a staff PIN. The PIN is stored in the device Keychain and is not synced to iCloud. The user can optionally unlock these screens with Face ID or Touch ID. Biometric authentication is handled by iOS, and BizNote does not receive biometric data.

## Data Collection

The developer does not operate a separate server for collecting user data from BizNote.

The developer cannot access the user's notes, business card data, scanned images, attachments, or iCloud data.

Advertising-related data processed by Google is described in the Advertising and Third-Party Services section below. The developer does not receive the user's notes or business card data through advertising.

## Advertising and Third-Party Services

BizNote shows ads provided by Google AdMob using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. A banner ad may appear at the bottom of the note screen, and a full-screen ad may appear when the user enters Event Mode, imports a promotional video in Event Mode, or exports the visitor list.

BizNote asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before the advertising identifier of your device can be used for ads. BizNote requests non-personalized ads only and does not use ad personalization, whether or not you allow tracking.

If you allow it, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may use your device's advertising identifier to deliver and measure ads and to limit how often they are shown. Apple's App Privacy information describes this as data used for tracking. If you do not allow it, ads are still shown, and all features of the app work the same.

You can change this choice at any time in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking.

To deliver ads, limit how often they appear, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance, Google may collect and process device and ad-related information such as the IP address, device identifiers, ad interactions, and performance and crash diagnostics. This information is collected by Google, not by the developer.

The user's notes, business card data, scanned images, attachments, and event visitor information are not shared with Google or any advertiser and are not used to select ads.

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and other regions where it is required are asked for their consent through Google's User Messaging Platform. Where available, users can review or change this choice in Settings under Ad Privacy Options.

For more information about how Google uses data from apps that use its services, see https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Apart from the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform, BizNote does not contain other advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

## Permissions

BizNote may request access to the camera to scan business cards.

BizNote may request access to photos so the user can select images for business cards or event information.

BizNote may request access to contacts so the user can import contact information, detect duplicate contacts, or save business card information to Contacts.

BizNote may request Face ID or Touch ID authentication so the user can unlock Event Mode management screens.

BizNote may request access to location while in use so the user can enter the current location into meeting or event notes.

BizNote may request access to calendars and reminders if the user chooses to sync events or tasks with Apple's Calendar or Reminders apps.

## Data Deletion

Users can delete notes, business card records, attachments, event information, Event Mode visitor information, and custom templates inside the app.

Users can also delete the app to remove local app data from the device. If iCloud sync is enabled, synced data may remain in the user's iCloud account according to Apple's iCloud data handling behavior.

# BizNote Support

BizNote helps users create business notes, scan business cards, manage event and meeting information, and export selected data.

## Contact

Support email: paccbet17@gmail.com

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does BizNote require an account?

No. BizNote does not require account registration or login.

### Where is my data stored?

Data is stored on your device. If iCloud sync is enabled, data may sync through your personal iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit service.

### Can the developer see my notes or business cards?

No. BizNote does not use a separate developer server for collecting notes, business cards, images, attachments, or iCloud data.

### Why does BizNote request camera access?

Camera access is used to scan business cards.

### Why does BizNote request photo access?

Photo access is used when you choose an image from your photo library for a business card or event information.

### Why does BizNote request contacts access?

Contacts access is used to import contact information, detect duplicates, or save business card information to the Contacts app.

### Why does BizNote request location access?

Location access is used only when you choose to insert your current location into a meeting or event note.

### Why does BizNote request calendar or reminders access?

Calendar and Reminders access is used only when you enable integration features for events or tasks.

### How do I delete my data?

Delete notes, business cards, attachments, event information, and custom templates inside the app. To remove local app data entirely, delete the app from the device.
